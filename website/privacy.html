Privacy notice

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This notice explains how Gensolve ("we", "us") handles personal data collected through this website (gensolve.in), where we present our product, NewVis AI. It is written to meet our obligations under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

Only what you type into the "Request a callback" form: your name, phone number, email address, and — if you choose — your organisation, site type and a message. When you submit, our server also records the submission time, your IP address and your browser's user-agent string, solely to prevent spam and abuse.

This website does not use analytics, advertising trackers or third-party cookies. Your browser stores two small flags locally so the callback form doesn't pop up repeatedly; they never leave your device.

Why we collect it

To contact you about your enquiry and to understand how NewVis AI could work for your site. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or add you to marketing lists without asking.

Where it is stored and for how long

Submissions are stored on servers we control. We keep an enquiry for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, then delete it, unless you become a customer (in which case it becomes part of our customer records) or ask us to delete it sooner.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or erase your data, or withdraw your consent to being contacted, at any time. Email us and we will act on it within 30 days.
