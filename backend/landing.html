Why NewVis

Where NewVis is different.

Uses cameras you already own

Works with your existing CCTV over standard RTSP. No forklift upgrade, no vendor lock-in on hardware.

Affordable & edge-first

Runs on modest on-site boxes — even CPU or USB AI accelerators — with no per-camera cloud fees. Raw video never leaves the building.

True cross-camera identity

Follows one person across cameras and floors as a single ID — and respects a real camera map, so no impossible "teleport" matches.

Privacy-first by design

People are tracked as anonymised IDs — no faces, no names — behind role-based access, licensing and an append-only audit log.

Plain-English, not raw logs

Ask "show falls on camera 3" and read a written summary of activity — built for staff, not data engineers.

Cost-optimised, accuracy-preserving

A stack of efficiency levers cuts compute without trading away detection — we don't ship anything that regresses accuracy.
